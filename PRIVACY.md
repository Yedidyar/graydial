# Privacy

GrayDial collects nothing.

- **No network access.** The app has no `INTERNET` permission and makes no network requests.
- **No analytics, telemetry, advertising, crash reporting or accounts.**
- **No personal data.** GrayDial does not access contacts, location, camera, microphone, messages, files or the screen contents. It never captures or monitors the screen.
- **Stored locally:**
  - whether grayscale is on;
  - your preferred intensity;
  - the last value the system accepted, with the device boot count and a timestamp.

  This data stays in GrayDial's private storage. It is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.
- **Shizuku.** GrayDial talks to Shizuku only on your device. Shizuku is a separate app with its own privacy policy.
- **Deleting your data.** Clear GrayDial's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app.
